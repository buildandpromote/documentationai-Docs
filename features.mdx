## Fixed-Price Domain Offers

Launch your domains into the marketplace with fixed-price listings. Set your terms, display details, and wait for buyer interest. Nothing transfers without your explicit approval.

<Callout kind="info">
  Currently in private beta for domains. Websites and other assets coming soon.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="List Your Domain" icon="plus">
    Navigate to your dashboard at `https://dashboard.example.com/assets`.
    
    Click "Add Asset" and select "Domain".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Price and Terms" icon="dollar-sign">
    Enter your domain name, fixed price, and any conditions like transfer method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="eye">
    Review and publish. Your listing appears to verified buyers immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contact and Negotiation Tools

Initiate secure conversations with asset owners. Propose counters, share NDAs, and close deals efficiently.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Express Interest" icon="heart">
    Click "Make Offer" on any listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Negotiate" icon="message-circle">
    Send messages with your proposal. Owners respond via encrypted channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize" icon="check-circle">
    Agree on terms, sign escrow agreement, and transfer assets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Expandable title="Advanced Negotiation Tips" default-open="false">
  Use our built-in templates for LOIs and counteroffers. Integrate with `https://api.example.com/v1/negotiations` for programmatic updates:
  
  ````javascript
  const response = await fetch('https://api.example.com/v1/negotiations', {
    method: 'POST',
    headers: { 'Authorization': `Bearer ${YOUR_TOKEN}` },
    body: JSON.stringify({
      assetId: 'domain-123',
      offer: { price: 5000, terms: 'Escrow via Escrow.com' }
    })
  });
  ````
</Expandable>
