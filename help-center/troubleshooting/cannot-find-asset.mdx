---
title: Cannot Find My Asset
description: What to do if a recently added asset is missing.
---

## Symptoms
- Asset not appearing in dashboard
- Search returns no results

## Possible Causes
- Asset creation failed
- Filters are hiding your asset

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Filters">
    Make sure no date/type filters are enabled in your asset list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh Dashboard">
    Reload the dashboard to update the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-add Asset">
    If missing, try adding the asset again. Confirm all required fields.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Still can’t find it? Contact support@untappedasset.com
</Callout>
