## Quick Answer

To add an asset:

1. Log in to your Untapped Asset dashboard.
2. Click the **Add Asset** button at the top right.
3. Fill in the asset details (name, type, location).
4. Optionally, upload related files or documentation.
5. Hit **Save** to finish.

Assets are now visible in your assets list and can be managed or updated anytime.
