## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Visit https://app.untappedasset.com and sign in with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Asset">
    Locate the **Add Asset** button in the dashboard header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    Fill in basic details: name, type, and any notes about the asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: Attach Files">
    Upload supporting files or documentation if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Asset">
    Click **Save**. Your asset now appears in your list and is ready to track.
  </Step>
</Steps>
